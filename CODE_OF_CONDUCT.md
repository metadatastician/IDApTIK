# IDApTIK Code of Conduct

IDApTIK welcomes contributors and players of every background and level of
experience. We commit to maintaining a respectful, harassment-free community
in the repository, discussions, development spaces, events, and other
project-managed forums.

## Expected behaviour

- Be friendly, patient, and considerate.
- Welcome different perspectives, identities, abilities, and experience.
- Give constructive technical feedback without attacking the person.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and requests to stop.
- Credit other people's work and accept responsibility for mistakes.
- Prefer clear, accessible language and help others participate.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, stalking, sexualised attention,
deliberate intimidation, doxing, sustained disruption, plagiarism,
retaliation, and insulting or derogatory conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to
[developer@joshuajewell.dev](mailto:developer@joshuajewell.dev). Include the
relevant people, context, links or evidence, and any immediate safety concern.
The maintainer will handle reports discreetly, avoid unnecessary disclosure,
recuse where a conflict exists, and apply a proportionate response ranging
from correction or warning to temporary or permanent exclusion.

Questions that do not involve a private conduct report can be raised in
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/metadatastician/IDApTIK/discussions).

This policy is informed by Contributor Covenant 2.1 and the Speak Up! project.
