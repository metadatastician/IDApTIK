/* WHAT THIS IS */
An asymmetric two-player infiltration game. One player moves a body through a
facility; the other manipulates the grounded network simulation that facility
runs on; the guard is an NPC who interprets what it senses through its own
belief, interest and affect model. Winning is misdirection, not stealth.
Gameplay truth is a deterministic, event-sourced, engine-agnostic simulation in
crates/idaptik-core. Bevy renders it. Nothing render-side feeds back in.

/* COMPONENTS */

Graphical frontend: crates/idaptik-bevy (Bevy, 2.5D side-on, thin)
