= Observer-relative evidence ledger
:toc:

Status: design draft v0.2. The structures and policies below are proposed
contracts, not a claim of production implementation or proof.

== Purpose and boundary

The ledger represents what one observer can justify from available evidence.
It preserves ignorance, competing explanations, source provenance, conflict,
time and revision. It never consults hidden world truth while constructing an
observer's state.

The ledger is not the world model, a planner or a truth oracle. It supplies
queries and provenance-bearing changes to the epistemic state. A profile policy
may use those queries when proposing an action; the ledger does not choose or
execute the action.

IDApTIK owns its current fixtures and game policy. Host-neutral ledger and
reduction semantics move to Enaction Engine only under ADR-0010's extraction
rule. UMS may author and validate a versioned ledger profile but is not a
runtime dependency or semantic authority.

== Frame of discernment

Each finite-frame ledger declares the hypotheses for one question:

[source,text]
----
Frame {
  frame_id,
  version,
  hypotheses: [HypothesisId],
  open_world: bool,
  exclusivity: Exclusive | Overlapping,
  unknown_id: optional HypothesisId
}
----

Example route-attribution hypotheses are `front-door`, `ventilation` and
`unknown`. If a frame is incomplete, mass remains on a set of alternatives or
on unknown; it is never silently redistributed to named hypotheses.

A frame does not automatically cover an open-ended relational proposition
language. Evidence outside declared frames and the interaction between
miniKanren variables and finite focal sets remain open design questions.

== Basic belief assignment

For deterministic replay, mass uses integer units:

[source,text]
----
Mass = uint32 in 0..10_000

Bpa {
  frame_id,
  focal_sets: [(FocalSet, Mass)],
  uncommitted_mass: Mass,
  provenance: [EvidenceId]
}
----

A focal element is a sorted, duplicate-free set of hypothesis IDs. The
invariants are:

. every hypothesis belongs to the declared frame;
. focal sets are canonicalized before comparison;
. focal masses plus uncommitted mass equal 10,000;
. an empty focal set is forbidden in normalized state and retained only as
  explicit conflict during combination;
. serialization order is canonical.

`Bel(A)` sums masses wholly contained in `A`. `Pl(A)` sums masses whose focal
sets intersect `A`, including mass that has not ruled `A` out. A policy retains
the interval `[Bel(A), Pl(A)]` until it explicitly requests a point decision.

== Evidence items

[source,text]
----
Evidence {
  evidence_id,
  observer,
  source,
  observed_at,
  received_at,
  reliability: Mass,
  freshness: Mass,
  bpa,
  observation_kind,
  supporting_trace_events,
  expires_at,
  independence_group,
  notes
}
----

Observation and receipt time are separate so a delayed report cannot appear to
have been known earlier. `independence_group` prevents double-counting two
reports derived from the same camera event.

Reliability discounts an evidence item rather than rewriting the observer's
prior. Discounted mass moves to unknown or uncommitted mass. Reliability comes
from a named source policy and is never learned silently from the hidden answer.

== Time and revision

Evidence is append-only and the current view is derived. Expiry or decay emits
a new derived event; it does not delete history. Recommended events are:

* `EvidenceReceived`
* `EvidenceDiscounted`
* `EvidenceCombined`
* `EvidenceConflictDetected`
* `HypothesisIntervalChanged`
* `EvidenceExpired`
* `BeliefRevisionRecorded`

Decay is a named, versioned policy using deterministic integer arithmetic.

== Combination and conflict

Combination policy is part of the versioned ledger profile:

* `IndependentConjunctive`
* `Cautious`
* `Disjunctive`
* `SourcePriority`
* `RetainConflict`

Highly conflicting evidence is not automatically normalized with Dempster's
rule. Conflict mass remains observable and produces a diagnostic. Policy may
discount, separate, escalate or gather more evidence.

Conflict is useful game state: high conflict can justify split coverage or
corroboration, while low conflict can justify committing resources. The action
remains a profile-policy decision rather than a ledger result.

== ESM and decision interfaces

[source,text]
----
observation
  -> EvidenceReceived
  -> ledger query (belief, plausibility, conflict, unknown)
  -> epistemic-state event
  -> supervisory proposal
  -> policy and verification
  -> candidate action event
----

The query surface is:

[source,text]
----
EvidenceQuery {
  frame_id,
  proposition,
  belief,
  plausibility,
  conflict,
  unknown,
  supporting_evidence,
  contrary_evidence
}
----

Example thresholds belong to a named profile policy, never to the ledger
kernel. A policy might preserve front-door coverage at a belief threshold,
assign an operator to vents at a plausibility threshold, or gather more
evidence when conflict is high.

== Deterministic encoding and estate seams

Canonical encoding sorts frame IDs, focal-set members and evidence IDs; uses
fixed-point mass; includes explicit policy and version fields; and never relies
on map iteration order. Combine, discount, decay and query operations require
replay, overflow and rounding tests.

Cross-language implementations follow the estate seam policy: Idris2 owns the
ABI contract, Zig implements the unified hexadeca-compatible FFI, and the C
header is generated. Any future BEAM-hosted native computation runs as a SNIF
inside the approved WebAssembly sandbox, never as a raw NIF. Rust components
are Rust/Crusoe components. These are boundary requirements, not claims that a
ledger ABI, FFI or SNIF exists today.

Proof claims require confirmed checker evidence for the named property.
Scaffolding, signatures, types and workflows are recorded as scaffolding until
they prove and exercise the stated obligation. Gaps remain explicit.

== Conformance fixtures

The first fixtures cover:

. USB interest that rationally but incorrectly raises `wants-usb`;
. fridge-note evidence that revises the earlier attribution;
. vent/front-door evidence with mass on both alternatives;
. two reports derived from one camera event, proving no double-counting;
. delayed evidence that cannot affect an earlier decision;
. high-conflict reports that retain conflict;
. open-world mass that remains unknown;
. snapshot and replay equivalence;
. stable tie-breaking and canonical serialization.

== Promotion criteria

The ledger does not become a shared public runtime contract until fixtures
demonstrate deterministic replay, provenance-complete revision, bounded focal
growth and memory, explicit conflict handling, observer-relative information
boundaries and decisions explainable entirely from ledger queries and declared
policy.
